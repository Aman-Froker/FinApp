# FinApp roadmap

Written 25 October 2026 from the PRD v1 (Shaurya, 25 October 2026) and the decisions taken in this session. Nothing below is built until this document is approved.

## 1. Product definition

An Android app that reads bank, card and UPI SMS and tells the user what they can safely spend today, with zero setup. Day one earns trust with four insight cards from the user's own history (Money Wrapped). Every morning it pushes one number. Every Sunday it runs a 60 second game that doubles as training data. Each week it asks for one more piece of data, only after proving value with what it already has.

Three additions agreed on top of the PRD:

1. **The budget is inferred, never asked.** There are no budgets to set and no salary form. After the history scan the app proposes the monthly budget itself: "Your budget looks like ₹62,000 a month, paid around the 1st", with the lines it used (salary credits found, recurring credits, or when no salary credit exists, the median monthly spend of the last three to six months and the most common spend-reset date). The figure is computed from the user's data, not generated by a model; the model only phrases the explanation. One tap accepts it; editing it is always available from the breakdown screen.
2. **Parser rules start from open source.** The rules pack is seeded from MIT licensed work and extended against our own corpus (section 6).
3. **Email alerts use the same parser.** Bank and card alert emails are text in the same formats as SMS. Users forward them to a personal address and the same rules parse them. Gmail OAuth comes later, behind the same source interface.

Decisions carried from the PRD review:

- **Local first.** Parsing, storage and every calculation run on the phone in SQLite. The server holds only the parse fallback, the shared merchant map, product events and the delete-all endpoint. Raw SMS never leave the phone; unparsed financial SMS are sent with digits masked.
- **Anonymous identity in v1.** No sign up before the SMS prompt. The device gets a random identity on first launch. The email and password auth built in Phase 3 stays and is offered only when a feature needs an account that outlives the device (backup, Gmail OAuth, Account Aggregator).
- **Expo development build, not bare.** The SMS reader, broadcast receiver and notification listener are one small Expo native module in Kotlin with a config plugin. Everything from Phases 2 and 3 is kept.
- **Fallback model.** Claude Haiku through the API's provider interface, with a per user monthly cap on fallback calls and one call per message template, not per message.
- **Play Store first.** The SMS declaration, the privacy policy and an internal testing track ship in milestone 0, because rejection is the one risk that stops the product.

Out of scope for v1: manual entry, CSV import, category budgets, the conversational assistant, moving money, credit products, iOS.

## 2. Build order

Each milestone ends with a demo on the Android emulator and on one real phone with real SMS. Weeks are estimates for one developer plus Shaurya on rules and copy.

| Milestone | Weeks | Ships | Exit criterion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M0 Foundations | 1 | Native SMS module and dev build, SQLite schema, anonymous identity, events endpoint, delete-all, parser contract and corpus harness | Sideloaded APK reads the inbox on a real phone, and `pnpm test` runs the corpus |
| M1 Parser and history | 1 to 2 | Rules pack for the top 10 banks and 4 UPI apps, LLM fallback, salary detection, recurring detection, history parse under 30 s for 5,000 SMS | Rules parse 90 percent or more of the corpus for each top 10 sender; fallback covers the rest within cap |
| M2 Money Wrapped | 1 | Onboarding flow (welcome, permission, progress, salary confirm or ask, four cards, notification opt in), card scoring | All six card types render from real history; fewer than three qualifying cards shows the summary card |
| M3 Daily number and alerts | 1 to 2 | Home screen number, pace line, tap through breakdown with editable lines, 8 AM local notification, six alerts with the two a day cap, live SMS queue | Number recomputes on a new SMS; alerts fire from corpus replays; cap holds |
| M4 Give to get | 1 | Asks engine (triggers, declined cooldown of 14 days), label top 5 payees, notification listener for UPI merchant names, one goal and the goal adjusted number | Each ask appears only on its trigger and never within 14 days of a decline |
| M5 Sunday ritual | 1 | Guess Your Week, the four side games, Money IQ, streak with one freeze, goal jar visual, share card | Game answers write back to labels and predictions; share card never shows amounts |
| M6 Email alerts and cards | 1 | Personal forwarding address, inbound webhook, same parser on email text, card statement tracking and card due alert from email | A forwarded HDFC card statement email produces a card due alert |

Later, in this order: Account Aggregator (Setu or Finvu), Gmail OAuth receipts, the conversational assistant on top of the local data, category budgets and manual entry for users who want them, iOS with email and AA only.

## 3. Screens per milestone

- **M2**: Welcome, SMS permission, Parsing progress, Budget proposal (inferred figure, the lines behind it, accept or edit), Insight card (one component, six variants), Notification opt in.
- **M3**: Home (number, pace line, next salary date), Breakdown (income, committed, goal set aside, spent so far, days left; each line editable), Transactions list (grouped by day, filter chips), Transaction detail (recategorise, mark as transfer, mark as refund expected), Alert detail.
- **M4**: Ask card (one component, six variants), Label payees, Notification access explainer, Goal set up, Goal card on Home.
- **M5**: Sunday game flow (guess, reveal, confirm, predict), side game (four variants), Money IQ card, Share card.
- **M6**: Forwarding address screen with copy button and the Gmail filter instructions, Cards list, Card statement detail.
- **Settings** (M3): salary and pay day, notification time, data sources, delete all data.

Every screen renders only from the local database or a real empty state. Money Wrapped cards use the accent and positive tones only, never red.

## 4. Data model, on device (SQLite, expo-sqlite)

Amounts are integer paise. Dates are ISO strings in the device zone with the offset kept. Every table has `createdAt` and `updatedAt`.

| Table | Fields | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sms_raw` | id, sender, receivedAt, body, parsedStatus (pending, parsed, ignored, fallback), templateHash | Live receiver appends here; the app parses on open. Non financial senders are dropped before insert |
| `transactions` | id, smsId, emailId, date, amountMinor, direction (debit, credit), accountId, instrument (upi, card, nach, atm, imps, neft, other), counterparty, counterpartyVpa, merchantId, category, status (success, failed, refund, reversed), kind (spend, income, transfer, fee, refund), source (rule, llm, user), referenceNo, balanceAfterMinor | `kind` is what the formula uses; card bill payments and self transfers are `transfer`, never spend. `referenceNo` plus amount plus date dedupes SMS and email copies of the same event |
| `accounts` | id, last4, kind (bank, card, wallet), bankCode, isSalaryAccount, label | Created from parsed SMS; the user names them later |
| `balances` | accountId, balanceMinor, asOf | Last seen balance per account, from SMS that carry it |
| `recurring` | id, counterparty, amountMinor, cadence (monthly, weekly), nextDue, type (emi, sip, autopay, rent, subscription, salary), lastSeenAmountMinor, active | Detected per the PRD rule; NACH and autopay registration SMS create a row on first sight |
| `card_statements` | id, accountId, totalDueMinor, minDueMinor, dueDate, paidAt, source | From statement SMS or email; `paidAt` set when a payment SMS matches |
| `labels` | vpa, merchantName, category, labelledBy (user, notification, shared), confidence | Local view of the shared map plus the user's own labels |
| `salary` | amountMinor, payDay, confirmedAt, source (detected, user) | One row. The budget envelope |
| `goals` | id, name, amountMinor, targetDate, monthlySetAsideMinor, active | One active goal in v1 |
| `insights` | id, type, computedAt, score, payload, viewedAt, actionTakenAt | The Day 1 cards and their outcomes |
| `alerts` | id, type, triggerKey, scheduledFor, sentAt, suppressedAt | Enforces the two a day cap and the suppression rules |
| `asks` | id, type, triggeredAt, shownAt, acceptedAt, declinedAt | Enforces "never before value" and the 14 day cooldown |
| `game_answers` | id, week, game, category, guessMinor, actualMinor, predictionMinor, worthIt, answeredAt | Guess Your Week and side games |
| `sender_stats` | sender, seen, parsedByRule, parsedByLlm, failed, windowStart | Flags a sender whose rule parse rate drops below 90 percent |
| `settings` | key, value | notification time, notification access state, onboarding step |

## 6. Parser contract and rules pack

**Input**: `{ sender, body, receivedAt, channel: 'sms' | 'email' }`. Email bodies are reduced to plain text before parsing.

**Output**: the PRD parser object, in paise: `{ type, amountMinor, accountLast4, instrument, counterparty, counterpartyVpa, balanceAfterMinor, date, status, referenceNo, confidence }`, or `null` when the message is not a transaction.

**Rules pack**: one file per sender in `packages/parser/rules/<bank>.ts`, each a list of named patterns with the fields they capture, plus a shared normaliser (amount formats, date formats, VPA shapes). The pack is pure TypeScript with no React Native dependency so it runs on device, on the server and in Vitest.

**Corpus**: `packages/parser/corpus/<bank>.jsonl`, one masked SMS per line with the expected output. Every rule change runs the whole corpus. The target is 90 percent per sender for the top 10 banks (HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis, Kotak, PNB, BoB, IDFC First, Yes, IndusInd) and the UPI apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, CRED).

**Open source seed**, checked on 25 October 2026:

| Project | Licence | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [transaction-sms-parser](https://github.com/saurabhgupta050890/transaction-sms-parser) (TypeScript, MIT, 60 stars) | MIT | Seed. Extracts account, balance, type, amount, reference and merchant for Axis, ICICI, Kotak, HDFC, IDFC, Federal and major cards and wallets. No SBI. Its README example mislabels the balance as the amount, so every pattern is re-verified against our corpus before use |
| [UPIPulse](https://github.com/Neilboss/UPIPulse) (TypeScript, MIT) | MIT | Patterns for SBI, PNB and the four UPI apps, and its normalise, prefilter, dedupe pipeline shape |
| [PennyWise AI](https://github.com/sarim2000/pennywiseai-tracker) (Kotlin, 602 stars) | AGPL 3.0 | Reference only for which formats exist across 57 institutions. Its code is not copied: AGPL would require releasing our app under AGPL |
| [transaction_sms_parser](https://pub.dev/packages/transaction_sms_parser) (Dart) | see package | Reference for the 95 UPI handle list and card scheme detection |

Shaurya owns the rules and corpus; the pipeline, harness and fallback are built in M0 and M1 so both can proceed in parallel from the contract above.

## 7. API endpoints for v1

| Method and path | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `POST /v1/devices` | Register an anonymous device, returns a device token |
| `POST /v1/parse/fallback` | Masked unparsed message in, parser object out; rate and cost limited per device |
| `GET /v1/merchants?vpa=` and `POST /v1/merchants/labels` | Read the shared map, contribute a label |
| `POST /v1/events` | Batch of product events |
| `POST /v1/inbound/email` | Webhook from the inbound email provider; verified by signature; forwards parsed alerts to the device by push |
| `DELETE /v1/devices/me` | Delete all server data for the device |

All existing auth routes remain for the later account features.

## 8. Formulas, as they will be coded

- Safe today = (income − committed − goal set aside − spent so far) ÷ max(days left to next salary, 1). On the last day the cycle total is shown instead of a per day figure.
- Income = confirmed salary + recurring credits seen in 2 of the last 3 months. With no salary found, the user supplied take home.
- Committed = active recurring debits not yet paid this cycle, including the card bill when it is paid from this salary.
- Spent so far = `kind = spend` debits since the last salary credit; card swipes count on the swipe date.
- Insight score = log10(annual rupees) × surprise weight × actionable, weights and thresholds per the PRD.
- Money IQ = mean accuracy of the last four guesses, accuracy = max(0, 100 − 100 × |guess − actual| ÷ actual).
- Recurring = same counterparty, amount within 10 percent, two or more occurrences 25 to 35 days apart (monthly) or 6 to 8 days apart (weekly).

## 9. Product events

permission_sms_prompted, permission_sms_granted, history_parsed (count, seconds, ruleRate), salary_confirmed, salary_asked, insight_viewed (type, position), insight_action_taken, number_opened, breakdown_opened, line_edited, alert_sent, alert_opened, ask_shown, ask_accepted, ask_declined, game_started, game_completed (accuracy), share_card_created, email_forwarded, delete_requested.

## 10. Risks carried into the plan

- Play review of READ_SMS and notification access: declaration filed in M0; the app must work with SMS only and with neither permission (empty states that explain what is missing).
- Background execution on Xiaomi, Oppo and Vivo: the live SMS path stores to the queue natively and parses on open; the morning number is scheduled locally the evening before and corrected on open.
- Self transfers and card bill payments inflating spend: pairing by amount and time, and the user can mark any transaction as a transfer from the breakdown.
- Bank format drift: sender parse rate stats surface in Settings and a server alert fires below 90 percent.
- Recurring detection needs two occurrences, so subscriptions started in the last 30 days are missed on Day 1.

## 11. Decisions and open questions

Decided on 25 October 2026:

1. **Name**: Fin App for now. Domain still open; it is needed before email forwarding goes live in M6.
2. **Play Console**: deferred. M0 drops the Play internal track and declaration; testing is by sideloaded APK. The declaration becomes the first task once a Play account exists.
3. **Inbound email**: Cloudflare Email Routing with an Email Worker, which is free and posts each message to `POST /v1/inbound/email`. It needs the domain on Cloudflare. For development, `pnpm --filter @finapp/api replay-email <fixture> <address>` posts saved bank emails to the same signed webhook, so M6 is not blocked by the domain. Open source fallback if Cloudflare is ruled out: a self-hosted Haraka SMTP server.
   Going live (built in M6, not yet deployed): put the domain on Cloudflare; set the API's `INBOUND_DOMAIN` and a 32+ character `INBOUND_SECRET`; deploy `infra/email-worker` with `wrangler deploy` after `wrangler secret put INBOUND_SECRET` and setting `API_URL`; add an Email Routing catch-all rule sending to the Worker. Push delivery to the phone needs a Firebase project; until then the phone collects email results on each refresh.
4. **Salary**: no ask. The budget is inferred from the scan and proposed for one-tap acceptance (section 1, item 1).
5. **Notification time**: fixed 8 AM.

Still open, owned by Shaurya:
6. Shaurya's rules format: TypeScript pattern files in the repo, or a JSON format edited outside it? The harness supports either; TypeScript gives types and tests for free.
7. Fallback budget: cap of fallback calls per device per month. Proposal: 50, which at Haiku pricing is under one rupee per user.
8. Which four UPI apps for the notification listener in M4: Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm and CRED, or include BHIM and Amazon Pay?
